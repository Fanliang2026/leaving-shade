Preface

Five years have passed since my mother-in-law left us.

For almost as long, I have wanted to write about her. Each time I sat down to begin, however, I found myself setting the pen aside. It wasn't because there was too little to say, but because there was far too much. A life as full as her resists being contained by words.

Even now, I know these pages cannot fully capture the warmth, resilience, humor, and quiet strength that made her who she was. But perhaps they can preserve something just as important: the traces she left in the lives of those who knew and loved her.

Looking back, her life unfolded much like a full-length play—an opening marked by hardship, moments of joy and heartbreak, unexpected turns, and, at last, the final curtain.
